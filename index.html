Frequently asked questions

Does QuarterJar link to my bank account?
No. You log income and deposits yourself. QuarterJar never connects to any bank or financial account.
Is the recommended set-aside amount official tax advice?
No. QuarterJar's numbers are an estimate based on the IRS safe harbor rules and the figures you enter. Always confirm with a licensed accountant before filing.
What happens to my data if I delete the app?
Since everything is stored locally on your device, deleting QuarterJar deletes your data with it. There is no cloud backup or account to recover from.
How do I cancel my Premium subscription?
Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.
Which states does QuarterJar cover?
QuarterJar's calculations cover federal estimated tax only. State estimated-tax rules vary and are not included.
