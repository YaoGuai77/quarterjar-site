QuarterJar Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

QuarterJar does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your personal or financial data. This page explains exactly what that means.

Data storage

Everything you enter in QuarterJar — income entries, deposits, your tax profile, and payment history — is stored locally on your device, inside an Apple App Group container shared only between the QuarterJar app and its Home Screen widget. None of this data is sent to any server, because QuarterJar does not operate one.

Purchases

QuarterJar Premium subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. QuarterJar never sees or stores your payment information. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles payment and subscription data.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, QuarterJar schedules local notifications directly on your device using Apple's notification framework. No data leaves your device to make this work.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

QuarterJar does not include any analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is being collected.

Children's privacy

QuarterJar is a financial planning tool intended for self-employed adults filing U.S. taxes. It is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised effective date above.
